# Changelog

All notable public changes to RIEC Guard are recorded here. This file describes the local Build
Week release candidate; it does not claim that an external release, deployment, or submission has
occurred.

## Build Week 2026 submission candidate — 2026-07-20

### Added

- A run-bound grouped RIEC-L1 engine over the fixed M0–M6 candidate library.
- Empirical, Gaussian, and Student-t headroom protocols; whole-deployment-group bootstrap;
  ordered-stability and evidence-sufficiency gates; and a deterministic six-state action engine.
- Three deterministic public synthetic mechanisms with production-default recorded results:
  stable agreement, heavy-tail conflict, and ordered drift.
- A bounded GPT-5.6 workflow for advisory column-role suggestions, evidence-linked explanation,
  and deterministic-first claim auditing.
- A public Streamlit product with recorded and explicit recomputation modes, offline fixture GPT,
  optional server-enabled live GPT, evidence views, and sanitized downloads.
- Release documentation, judge instructions, submission/video drafts, deterministic release
  auditing, and fast release-layer tests.

### Safety and provenance

- Numerical results and actions remain owned by deterministic code.
- Raw rows are excluded from the GPT context.
- Public synthetic data are separated from private industrial sources and adapters.
- The pre-existing RIEC-L1 research concept is distinguished from Build Week product work in
  `docs/BUILD_WEEK_PROVENANCE.md`.
- The demo remains retrospective screening only: no production setpoint, safety/compliance
  determination, causality result, or achieved-savings claim.
