Target final duration: **2:35–2:55**, never more than three minutes. The shot

## Recording preparation

- Use a clean browser window at 1440×900 or higher and set zoom so the selected
  result, controls, and headings remain legible.
- Close personal tabs and disable desktop, browser, calendar, chat, and email
  notifications.
- Use fixture-only mode by default. Do not expose secrets, environment values,
  terminal keys, local paths, or personal account details.
- Preload the stable scenario and verify the three scenario results before recording.
- Generate the stable fixture memo once during rehearsal, then clear it before the
  final take so the click is visible.
- Prepare a public README or `docs/BUILD_WEEK_PROVENANCE.md` preview for the Codex
  shot. Do not show a private Codex conversation.
- Use deliberate cursor motion, avoid rapid scrolling, and pause briefly on every
  action headline and interval.
- Record clean English narration without copyrighted music.
- Export once, inspect every cut for private information, and confirm the final
  duration is under three minutes.

## Optional live-footage rule

Live footage is optional and may replace shots 7–8 only after a human-authorized run
succeeds without exposing a credential or personal account detail. Show the
acknowledgement and one deliberate live button click, then use the optional replacement
sentence from `VIDEO_SCRIPT.md`. If configuration is absent, the request fails, the
audit does not complete, or any sensitive value could appear, use the offline fixture
shots without apology or delay.

## Final edit checklist

- The order is title, deterministic/GPT split, stable action, stable evidence,
  heavy-tail conflict, drift with no interval, memo, claim audit, public Codex
  provenance, and closing product screen.
- Every scenario label, action, and interval is legible and matches the script.
- The public synthetic-data statement and retrospective-screening limitations are
  spoken.
- No unsupported savings, optimization, safety, compliance, or production-setpoint
  claim appears in narration, captions, or background tabs.
- No secret, personal tab, private conversation, terminal key, local path, or
  unlicensed media is visible or audible.
- Final duration is under three minutes with clear English narration.
