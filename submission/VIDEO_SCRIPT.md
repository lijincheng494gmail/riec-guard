Operational teams face conflicting statistical views, yet someone still needs a
defensible next action. RIEC Guard turns that disagreement into an auditable
retrospective screening decision.

---

Deterministic Python profiles data, evaluates grouped RIEC-L1 candidates, runs tail
protocols and gates, chooses the action, and hashes evidence. GPT-5.6 never
calculates numbers or chooses the action. It explains verified aggregates and
audits wording.

---

This demo uses public synthetic data only. The stable symmetric process opens by
default. Its dataset-bound record uses 200 whole-group bootstrap replicates. The
decision is Controlled pilot range supported, with a 0.15 to 0.40 milliliter
retrospective screening reference. In Evidence, H1, H2, H3, and U1 are visible
separately, alongside G1 stability, G2 sufficiency, the grouped RIEC winner, and the
RIEC-to-protocol-to-action evidence chain.

---

Now I switch to heavy-tail particulate variation. The same policy and decision
pipeline finds material disagreement between the empirical, Gaussian, and
Student-t views. RIEC Guard preserves that conflict instead of averaging it away.
The action becomes Conservative pilot only, with a narrower 0.05 to 0.14
milliliter retrospective reference. This still requires a controlled pilot and
engineering review.

---

The batch-drift scenario tells a different story. Ordered stability is materially
concerning, so the state becomes Diagnose process first. Notice that the interface
shows no pilot interval. Zero-to-zero is never presented as a recommendation.

---

Back in GPT-5.6, I generate the evidence-linked memo and claim audit. This offline fixture demonstrates the structured workflow without making an API request. The
memo cites verified aggregate evidence, while deterministic checks reject unknown
numbers, unsupported action language, and prohibited claims before the final audit.
GPT-5.6 is the bounded narrative and claim-audit layer. If it is unavailable, the
statistical decision remains usable.

---

Codex implemented and tested the working product against the supplied specification.
One valid blocker caught that 192 rows could not satisfy the frozen 1% tail
threshold. The human retained the threshold and increased the benchmark to 1,056
rows. Codex did not create the underlying research method.

---

RIEC Guard makes disagreement inspectable: deterministic statistics decide,
GPT-5.6 explains and audits, and results stay evidence-linked. This is retrospective
screening—not production optimization, safety, compliance, causality, or an
achieved-savings claim.
